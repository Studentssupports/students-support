DOWN UNDER — AUSTRALIA ARRIVAL GUIDE

HOW TO OPEN THE WEBSITE
1. Download the ZIP file.
2. Right-click the ZIP file and choose Extract All.
3. Open the extracted folder.
4. Double-click index.html to open it in your browser.
5. Keep index.html, style.css and script.js together in the same folder.

WHAT IS INCLUDED
- Responsive layouts for desktop, laptop, tablet and mobile screens.
- A working home-page search with live word suggestions. Choose a suggestion or press Enter to open the matching section. Use the up/down arrow keys to move through suggestions and Escape to close them.
- An Important Words page with plain-English explanations, steps and official links for Opal cards, bank accounts, SIM/eSIM, TFN, ABN, myGov, OSHC, emergency number 000, rent/bond, public transport and student visa conditions.
- Tourist destination cards with expandable attraction photos, descriptions and links.
- Arrival checklists and tips.

NOTES
- The website stores checklist progress and tip preferences in this browser on this device.
- Internet access is needed for online photos, fonts and external official links.
- Some services and eligibility rules depend on your visa, state or personal circumstances. Check the official links for current information.
- To publish it online with GitHub Pages, upload all three website files into the same repository folder.
